UNDERSTAND C1

What does C1 English mean?

C1 is an advanced level of English. At this level, learners can understand complex texts, communicate effectively and express ideas with a high degree of fluency and precision.

Vocabulary is only one part of C1 English. You should also develop grammar, reading, listening, speaking and writing skills.

ADVANCED WORDS

C1 vocabulary collection

Don't just memorize the definition. Learn how each word behaves in a real sentence.

VERB

Acquire

To gain or obtain something, especially through effort or experience.

Example: She acquired valuable skills during her internship.

ADJECTIVE

Ambiguous

Having more than one possible meaning or interpretation.

Example: The instructions were too ambiguous to follow.

ADJECTIVE

Compelling

Extremely convincing or interesting.

Example: The researcher presented a compelling argument.

VERB

Diminish

To become or make something less important, effective or noticeable.

Example: Lack of sleep can diminish concentration.

ADJECTIVE

Inevitable

Certain to happen and impossible to avoid.

Example: Some degree of change is inevitable.

ADJECTIVE

Substantial

Large in amount, size or importance.

Example: The project requires a substantial investment.

VERB

Undermine

To gradually weaken someone's confidence, authority or effectiveness.

Example: Constant criticism can undermine a student's confidence.

ADJECTIVE

Sophisticated

Highly developed, advanced or complex.

Example: The company developed a sophisticated security system.

VERB

Facilitate

To make an action or process easier.

Example: Technology can facilitate communication.

ADJECTIVE

Coherent

Logical, clear and well organized.

Example: Her essay presented a coherent argument.

VERB

Allocate

To officially give a particular amount of money, time or resources to something.

Example: The school allocated more resources to science.

ADJECTIVE

Prevalent

Common or widespread in a particular place, group or situation.

Example: The problem is particularly prevalent among teenagers.

COLLOCATIONS

Learn words together

Advanced learners should learn common word combinations, not isolated vocabulary alone.

Significant impact

The new policy had a significant impact on university students.

Compelling evidence

The researchers discovered compelling evidence supporting the theory.

Considerable amount

The project required a considerable amount of preparation.

Highly effective

The technique proved to be highly effective.

STUDY METHOD

Don't memorize. Use the words.

STEP 01

Understand

Learn the definition, pronunciation and part of speech.

STEP 02

See it

Read several examples of the word being used naturally.

STEP 03

Use it

Create your own sentence using the word.

STEP 04

Review it

Return to the word later instead of trying to memorize everything in one session.
